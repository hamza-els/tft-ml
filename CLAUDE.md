## Things that will bite you

- **Everything is calibrated to one HUD layout**: BoxBox's stream, VOD `knB6dE8Rbgg`, TFT Set 18. Regions in `configs/vision_config.yaml`, `WATCH_REGIONS` in `frame_dedup.py`, and the templates in `assets/templates/hud/` are all specific to it. Another streamer or UI scale gives silent garbage or "no games detected". Run `preview_regions.py` before processing anything new.
- **A new TFT set** needs `fetch_game_data.py --set ...`, new icon templates, and changes the state vector's size (champion and trait counts).
- **The HUD is always the player's own, the board is not.** About 28% of frames show another player's board (away fights, scouting). `home_board` marks own-board frames: the augment bar on the left only means own board, on the right only means someone else's (scouting). **In combat both bars show** (board owner's on the left, visitor's on the right), so then, and with no bar, the arena colour decides (`home_board_rows`). Never read units or augments off a frame that isn't `home_board`.
- **The augment bar's count must be known before cutting icons**: cutting 2 icons as 3 gives crops straddling two icons (Hamza found many in the labeler, 2026-11-19). `AugmentReader` (`src/vision/augments.py`) tries every count the stage allows (`expected_counts`: at least the picks done, 2-1/3-2/4-2; up to 4: a 4th augment shows up in some games, and extras are common, e.g. 3 at 3-2) at every centre within ±20 px of the calibrated one (8vvYT3TbJTw sits ~15 px right; another player's bar moves with the camera), and keeps the best fit: dark outline inside, scenery just outside, icons centred in their slots, every slot with an icon over several middle rows. Dark scenery (vzUwGQ4GxFQ's forest) leaves only the icons' centring to decide; a dark panel sliding over the bar (only icon tops show) reads 0. Checked by eye on 96 random bars across all 8 VODs: 93 cut right, the rest a bar mid-animation or under the XP bar. The old reader missed most other-player bars (403 of 2,280 sampled frames now found).
- **BoxBox's overlay lists each game's augments by name** (bottom left, under "Portal: ..."; `src/vision/augment_overlay.py`). ## Decisions already made (don't reopen without reason)

- Champion classes are names only, no star level in the label. Star level is read separately later.
- The shop is read by OCR of card names, not by a detector.
- Board and bench units are found by their health bar (`unit_reader.occupied`: a green bar lined up with the hex/slot with a star badge on its left; the badge rules out the Little Legend's nameplate bar), cut with a window anchored to that bar (`board.anchored`: fixed size, centred on the bar, top just above the badge), then classified. Hamza chose anchored windows over fixed ones (2026-11-18): held-out accuracy went from 91.5% to 93.7%. The YOLO path (`train_yolo.py`, `champion_detector.py`, `data/processed/labeled/`) predates this and is probably unnecessary.
- Other players' boards are ignored. Scouting is not modelled.
- Augment and item icons are matched to templates cut from footage and identified by ID (`aug_007`, `item_031`). Names are optional: the model only needs consistent IDs.
- No hard-coded thresholds in the decision logic; the Win Predictor's output is to be a model input.
- Frames stay on disk (full while space allows, else augment-only copies) until the unit classifier is really good (Hamza, 2026-11-20): every retrain means re-reading units from frames. After that, the plan is to keep only states plus a small augment crop store (about 30 icon crops per game and slot, ~2-3 MB per VOD, enough to rebuild templates and re-name games) and delete all frames. **2026-11-22, Hamza: "we now have enough frames": new VODs keep only the augment-only copies** (batch 3 runs with `--slim-frames --keep-frames-gb 100000`). The 14 VODs that still have full frames (~107 GB) keep them until the unit models are good (Hamza, 2026-11-22), then get slimmed; a VOD without full frames can't have its units re-read after a retrain.
